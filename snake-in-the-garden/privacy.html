Snake in the Garden: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Snake in the Garden ("the app") is made by Yusuf Bayindir. This policy explains what information is processed when you play the app.

Information we collect

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, skins and settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address and approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. On iOS you are asked for permission before your advertising identifier is used for tracking. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time.

Learn how Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings: on iOS under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and on Android under Settings › Google › Ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all game data stored on your device. You can also reset your progress in the app's Settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this address.
