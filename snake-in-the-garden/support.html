How do I play?

Swipe up, down, left or right to turn the snake. Eat apples to reveal gems, and collect all the gems shown at the top of the screen to clear the level. Don't bite your own tail or hit a wall!

What happens at the edge of the board?

The edges wrap around: leave one side and you come back on the opposite side. Only walls, your own body and extended thorns end the run.

How do power-ups work?

Power-ups appear by luck and vanish after 5 seconds, so grab them fast. Zoom speeds you up and doubles points, Snip shortens your tail, Magnet pulls food in, Ghost lets you pass through walls, Shield saves you from one crash, and more. Upgrade them in the Shop to make them last longer.

How do I get coins?

Eat apples, collect gems and coins on the board, earn stars, complete missions and claim the daily reward. You can also watch an optional ad in the Shop (up to 5 times a day).

Ads

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner during play and optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are always your choice.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it, and there is no cloud backup yet.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings (gear icon on the main menu) and tap Reset Progress twice.
