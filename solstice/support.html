Common questions

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. Solstice has no account and no server — your data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.

How do I cancel Solstice+? On your iPhone go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

How do I delete my data? In the app: Settings → Privacy Center → Delete All Data.
